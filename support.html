Help with Tiny Torque Machines

What do I need to play?

An iPhone or iPad running iOS 18 or later. Single-player races need no account and no connection.

How do I drive and fire?

Hold the double-chevron pedal to accelerate and the other pedal to brake, or to reverse once you've stopped. Steer with the on-screen wheel, or turn on tilt steering in Settings. Tap a weapon card to fire it. Settings can move the controls to the other side and resize them.

How do I pause, restart or leave a race?

Tap the pause button in the top-right corner, or press Menu on a game controller. The pause menu has Resume, Reset car, Restart, Settings and Quit. In a multiplayer race the others keep racing while your menu is open.

When do my weapons come back?

You arm four hardpoints in the Hardpoint Lab before a race. Each one rearms every time you complete a lap.

How does multiplayer work?

Choose Multiplayer on the main menu. It runs on Game Center, so everyone needs to be signed in to Game Center in the Settings app. Invite friends or find a race; empty seats are filled with computer drivers.

Can I use a controller, a keyboard or my TV?

Yes. Connect a game controller or a hardware keyboard and the game picks it up. To race on a TV or monitor, connect it with AirPlay or a cable; the phone becomes your controller.

Can I listen to my own music?

Yes. While another app is playing, the game's score waits and comes back when it stops; engines, weapons and the countdown still play over your music. Music and sound effects each have their own switch in Settings, and the ring/silent switch mutes the game.

Where is my progress saved?

Credits, cars, upgrades and paint are saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Is there anything for accessibility?

Settings has high-contrast cars, colour-blind-safe rival colours, left-handed controls and control sizing. With Reduce Motion on, the camera stops shaking and leaning into turns, and the speed streaks are switched off.
