Common questions

What do I need to play?

An iPhone or iPad running iOS 18 or later. No account is needed for single-player races.

How do I drive and fire?

Hold the double-chevron pedal to accelerate and the other pedal to brake or reverse. Steer with the on-screen wheel, or turn on tilt steering in Settings. Tap a weapon card to fire it. You can swap the controls to the other side, and resize them, in Settings.

How do I pause, restart or leave a race?

Tap the pause button in the top-right corner, or press Menu on a game controller. The pause menu has Resume, Reset car, Restart, Settings and Quit. In a multiplayer race the race keeps going while the menu is open.

How does multiplayer work?

Choose Multiplayer on the main menu. Multiplayer uses Game Center, so every player needs to be signed in to Game Center in the Settings app. Invite friends or find a race; empty seats are filled with computer-controlled cars.

Can I use a game controller, keyboard or my TV?

Yes. Connect a game controller or hardware keyboard and the game picks it up. To race on a TV or monitor, connect it with AirPlay or a cable; the phone becomes your controller.

Can I listen to my own music while I race?

Yes. Start your music first, or any time during play. The game's score steps aside while another app is playing and comes back when it stops; engines, weapons and the countdown still play over your music. You can turn the music and sound effects off separately in Settings, and the ring/silent switch mutes the game.

Where is my progress saved?

Your credits, cars, upgrades and paint are saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Is there anything for accessibility?

Settings has high-contrast cars, colour-blind-safe rival colours, left-handed controls and control sizing. The game respects Reduce Motion, which turns off camera shake, the camera's lean into turns and the speed streaks.
