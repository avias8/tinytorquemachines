What's in the box

Race, fight, build

Race

Three circuits: Bridge Ring's unguarded bridge over the water, Infinity Run's figure-eight with its overpass, and Star Valley's moonlit lake. Arcade handling made for drifting, and real starts under the light gantry.

Fight

Fourteen weapons across four hardpoints: missiles, lasers, plasma and a Quantum Torpedo up front; mines, oil and glue out the back. Damage you can see and hear, right up to the fireball.

Build

Eighteen cars to collect, from rally hatchbacks to a six-wheeler. Earn credits by finishing well, upgrade engine, handling and chassis, and repaint for free.

Race together

Invite friends or find a race through Game Center. Empty seats fill with bots, so every race has a full grid.
