Armoury

Every car has four hardpoints: three firing forward and one dropping things behind. Arm them in the Hardpoint Lab before the race; they rearm each time you complete a lap.

Forward-firing · 9

  • DisruptorMedium damage and passes through cars.
  • Quantum TorpedoTakes slopes, bounces, and deals high damage.
  • EMP MissileLow damage, but affects vehicle electronics.
  • SwarmerFires six mini-missiles per shot.
  • LaserA low-cost weapon with a wide hit zone.
  • MissileTakes any slope and deals high damage at low cost.
  • PlasmaMedium damage and bounces from track boundaries.
  • Homing MissileA guided missile designed for easy handling.
  • Turbo BlasterLow damage with a very high fire rate. 10 rounds a lap.

Dropped behind · 5

  • Cluster MineBursts into eight bouncing explosive pods.

  • Standard MineA straightforward explosive mine for holding the lead.

How you'll play

Touch. A steering wheel on one side, pedals and weapons on the other. Swap them for left-handed play and resize them to fit your hands.

Tilt. Steer by turning the phone; the on-screen wheel turns with you.

Controllers and keyboards. Pair a game controller or keyboard and it just works, menus included.

On the big screen. Connect a TV or monitor and the race goes there while the phone becomes your controller.

With friends. Invite friends or find a race through Game Center. Empty seats fill with computer drivers, so the grid is always full.

Your music. Start a playlist and the game's score steps aside; engines and explosions still come through.

Readable. High-contrast cars and colour-blind-safe rival colours are a switch away, and Reduce Motion calms the camera.

Smooth. Up to 120 frames a second on ProMotion displays.
