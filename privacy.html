Privacy

Effective November 21, 2026

Tiny Torque Machines is made by Avi Varma, an independent developer. This page explains what the game collects, why, and how long it is kept. In short: no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. The only information that leaves your device is what multiplayer needs to put you in a race.

Single-player

Single-player races collect nothing. Your progress (credits, cars, upgrades, paint) and your settings are saved on your device only. Deleting the app deletes them.

Multiplayer

Multiplayer is optional. When you use it, the game relies on two services.

Game Center, from Apple

Game Center signs you in, finds other players, and carries race data between the players in a race: car positions, weapon fire and results. Apple's handling of that data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

The race lobby

A small lobby server seats everyone in the same race. It runs on SpacetimeDB Maincloud, a hosted database service from Clockwork Labs. While you're in a multiplayer race it holds:

WhatWhyKept until
Your Game Center display nameSo the others can see who's in the lobbyAbout 30 seconds after you leave or lose connection
A random lobby identity the game createsSo a dropped connection doesn't lose your seatRemoved with your name; the key that proves it's yours stays in your device's keychain
Which race you're in, and whether you're ready and connectedTo start the race when everyone's readyYou leave
The race's settings: circuit, laps, start timeSo everyone races the same raceThe race empties

Your Game Center player ID is never stored on the lobby server. To match lobby seats with Game Center players, the game uses a one-way scrambled tag that means nothing outside that one lobby.

What the game doesn't do

  • No accounts, sign-ups or passwords.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software. Nothing is sold or shared for tracking.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Motion sensors

With tilt steering on, the game reads your device's motion sensors to steer. The readings are used on the device as you drive and are never stored or sent anywhere.

What Apple may share with me

If you've chosen to share analytics and crash data with app developers, Apple may give me anonymous, aggregated crash reports and usage figures through App Store Connect. They can't identify you, and you can turn sharing off in Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Analytics & Improvements.

Children

The game doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children beyond the multiplayer display name above, which comes from Game Center. Game Center's own controls, including Screen Time limits on multiplayer games, still apply.

Your choices

Play offline and nothing is collected. You can sign out of Game Center in the Settings app. To ask about anything the lobby holds, or to have it deleted, email me.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with a new effective date.
