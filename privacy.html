Privacy policy

Effective November 21, 2026

Tiny Torque Machines is made by Avi Varma, an independent developer. This policy explains what the game collects, why, and how long it is kept. The short version: no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. The only information that leaves your device is what multiplayer needs to put you in a race.

Single-player

Single-player races collect nothing. Your progress (credits, cars, upgrades, paint) and your settings are saved on your device only. Deleting the app deletes them.

Multiplayer

Multiplayer is optional. When you use it, the game uses two services:

Game Center (Apple)

Game Center signs you in, finds other players and carries race data between the players in a race, such as car positions, weapon fire and results. Apple's handling of Game Center data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

The race lobby (SpacetimeDB)

A small lobby server seats players in the same race. It runs on SpacetimeDB Maincloud, a hosted database service from Clockwork Labs. While you're in a multiplayer race it stores:

WhatWhyHow long
Your Game Center display nameTo show other racers who is in the lobbyRemoved about 30 seconds after you leave or disconnect
A random lobby identity, created by the gameTo keep your seat if your connection drops brieflyRemoved with your display name; the key that proves it is yours stays in your device's keychain
Your lobby status: which race, ready or not, connected or notTo start the race when everyone is readyRemoved when you leave
Race settings: track, laps, start timeSo every player races the same raceRemoved when the race empties

Your Game Center player ID is never stored on the lobby server. To match lobby seats to Game Center players, the game uses a one-way scrambled tag that only means something inside that one lobby.

What the game doesn't do

  • No accounts, sign-ups or passwords.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software, and no data sold or shared for tracking.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone access.

Motion sensors

If you turn on tilt steering, the game reads your device's motion sensors to steer the car. The readings are used on the device in real time and are never stored or sent anywhere.

Information Apple provides

If you choose to share analytics and crash data with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may give me anonymous, aggregated crash reports and usage statistics through App Store Connect. These can't identify you, and you can turn sharing off at any time in Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Analytics & Improvements.

Children

The game doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children beyond the multiplayer display name described above, which Game Center provides. Game Center's own controls, including Screen Time restrictions on multiplayer games, apply.

Your choices

You can play entirely offline and nothing is collected. You can sign out of Game Center in the Settings app. To ask about, or ask me to delete, anything the lobby holds, email me at the address below.
